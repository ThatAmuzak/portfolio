I started my CS degree in 2019. Arguably the last time the world was still sane.

The world was simple and predictable back then.
You did your DSA, you practiced some systems design.
A nice project or internship was a good bonus to strive for.
Climb up the corporate ranks, and you should be set for life.
These were the tenets sold to us by our seniors.

I learned coding the same way that most folks back, breaking my head over trivial logic and syntax, copying from Stack Overflow[^1], being stuck in tutorial hell for months.
Game development with Unity and C# was a genuinely good way for me to be excited about learning logic and code.
Building interactive experiences for people to pick up and play, making that digital character feel just right to move around and control, that's what gave me the motivation to keep building better systems.

Then a bat with a virus shut down the world.
By the time we recovered from that, a small research lab with a lofty goal released a prototype neural network for public preview.
